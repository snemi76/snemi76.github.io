Privacy policy

This OAuth app ("rclone") is used only by its owner, to mount and sync their own Google Drive with rclone on their own computer.

  • Files and data accessed through the Google Drive API stay on the owner's computer and in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, sold, or shared with any third party.
  • No analytics, cookies or tracking.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
